# VYTAL House NotebookLM Source Register

## Source Quality Rule

Use the most authoritative available source in this order:

1. VYTAL House approved internal document
2. Official federal, state, county, school, hospital, or institutional source
3. Signed lease, proposal, quote, insurance document, policy, or contract
4. Official association or economic-development source
5. Reputable research or industry source
6. News or secondary commentary only for context

Every uploaded item should have a clear file name, source owner, date accessed, and status: `verified`, `internal assumption`, `draft`, or `superseded`.

## D. Cybersecurity and Technology Sources

For each target employer collect:

1. Official company website
2. Maryland or regional office page
3. Careers and benefits page
4. Leadership or people-team page
5. Supplier or procurement page
6. Community and veteran-program page
7. Recent annual report or public company filing
8. Public workforce number when available
9. Public contact form
10. Public LinkedIn company page

Target categories:

- defense primes;
- national-security subcontractors;
- cybersecurity firms;
- managed security providers;
- cloud and data companies;
- software and AI firms;
- engineering organizations;
- technology staffing companies.

Do not upload scraped private emails, clearance information, employee lists, or nonpublic bid information.

## E. Healthcare Sources

Collect official pages from:

- regional hospitals and health systems;
- employee health and workforce wellbeing programs;
- nursing and healthcare associations;
- occupational-health providers;
- rehabilitation and physical-therapy organizations;
- medical staffing firms;
- physician and dental groups;
- public supplier-registration pages.

Add current sources on:

- healthcare-worker burnout and wellbeing;
- shift-work fatigue;
- employee-assistance and workforce wellbeing;
- privacy and HIPAA boundaries;
- employer wellness-program design.

Prioritize CDC, HHS, OSHA, NIOSH, CMS, and peer-reviewed research.

## F. First Responder and Corrections Sources

Collect:

- official police, fire, rescue, EMS, sheriff, and emergency-communications pages;
- public HR, wellness, occupational-health, training, union, or association contacts;
- Maryland corrections and detention-system public pages;
- county detention-center public information;
- procurement and vendor-registration instructions;
- official research on occupational stress, fatigue, sleep, and workforce wellbeing.

Do not upload security procedures, facility vulnerabilities, staff rosters, shift patterns, or restricted operational information.

## G. Athletics, Schools, and Minors Sources

Collect:

- official school-system athletic directories;
- school and district vendor-registration pages;
- athletic-director and athletic-trainer public directories;
- state athletic association rules;
- consent, safeguarding, emergency-action, concussion, and student-privacy policies;

- official sports-medicine and youth-safety guidance.

Any program involving minors must be separately reviewed for consent, supervision, insurance, privacy, and service eligibility.

Collect current official guidance relevant to the actual services offered:

- FDA device classification and labeling where applicable;
- Maryland professional-licensing requirements;

- ADA accessibility requirements;

## J. Recommended File Naming

Use:

`YYYY-MM-DD__SOURCE-OWNER__TOPIC__STATUS.ext`

Examples:

- `2026-07-31__VYTAL-HOUSE__Enterprise-Pricing__DRAFT.xlsx`
- `2026-07-31__FORT-MEADE__Tenant-Organizations__VERIFIED.pdf`
- `2026-07-31__SAM-GOV__Entity-Registration__VERIFIED.pdf`
- `2026-07-31__VENDOR-NAME__Equipment-Quote__CONFIDENTIAL.pdf`

## K. Do Not Upload to NotebookLM

- Protected health information
- Student records
- Social Security numbers
- Security-clearance details
- Controlled unclassified information
- Restricted facility information
- Passwords or API keys
- Confidential employee rosters
- Unredacted banking records
- Attorney-client privileged documents without authorization
- Personal contact data not needed for the project
- Outdated documents not marked superseded
