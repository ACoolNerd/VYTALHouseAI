# VYTAL House NotebookLM Build Instructions

## Recommended Notebook Structure

Create four separate notebooks rather than placing everything into one overloaded workspace.

### Notebook 1 — Executive Enterprise Growth

Upload:

- enterprise market strategy;
- financial projections;
- pricing model;
- 90-day launch plan;
- dashboard and KPI materials;
- approved business plan;
- current facility and operating assumptions.

Use for:

- executive report;
- investor or leadership briefing;
- financial analysis;
- audio overview;
- video overview.

### Notebook 2 — Fort Meade, Federal, Cyber, and Technology

Upload:

- Fort Meade official sources;
- SAM.gov and federal contracting sources;
- contractor and technology company sources;
- target-account research;
- government-contact and compliance rules;
- capability statement and procurement materials.

Use for:

- federal-market playbook;
- contractor target lists;
- sources-sought preparation;
- public/private pathway analysis;
- cyber and technology outreach.

### Notebook 3 — Healthcare, Public Safety, Corrections, Athletics, and Schools

Upload:

- official institutional directories;
- workforce wellbeing research;
- procurement pages;
- athletics and school policies;
- minor consent and safeguarding materials;
- first-responder and corrections sources;
- approved service eligibility rules.

Use for:

- vertical-specific outreach;
- pilot design;
- contact protocols;
- risk review;
- program eligibility.

### Notebook 4 — Sales Operations and CRM

Upload:

- contact protocol;
- outreach scripts;
- CRM exports;
- pipeline exports;
- visits log;
- proposals;
- meeting notes;
- pilot feedback;
- weekly KPI snapshots.

Use for:

- weekly pipeline brief;

- meeting preparation;
- follow-up messages;
- proposal development;
- quarterly business reviews.

## Upload Order

1. Approved VYTAL House internal overview
2. Latest strategy documents
3. Latest pricing and financial model
4. Current facility and capacity information
5. Official government and institutional sources
6. Target-employer sources
7. Compliance and claims sources
8. CRM and pipeline exports
9. Meeting notes and pilot feedback

## Source Hygiene

Before uploading:

- remove duplicates;
- mark superseded documents;
- redact sensitive information;
- convert images or scans into searchable PDFs when needed;
- place the date and status in the file name;
- confirm that public web sources are current;
- keep financial assumptions separate from verified actuals.

## First Outputs to Generate

1. Executive operating report
2. Fort Meade and contractor playbook
3. Three-year financial and capacity review
4. Enterprise sales playbook
5. Target-account register
6. 15–20 minute audio overview
7. 10–14 minute executive video overview
8. 90-day action register

## Quality-Control Prompt

After generating any major output, run:

“Audit this output against every uploaded source. Identify unsupported statements, missing citations, outdated facts, inconsistent financial assumptions, capacity conflicts, unsupported medical or ROI claims, implied government endorsements, privacy risks, and unresolved decisions. Produce a correction table with the original statement, issue, supporting source, recommended correction, and responsible owner. Do not rewrite the final output until the correction table is complete.”

## Final Leadership Review

Chauncey or the designated VYTAL House decision-maker should approve:

- pricing;
- public claims;
- target-account priorities;
- facility capacity assumptions;
- contracting entity;
- government-market route;
- clinical versus non-clinical boundaries;
- pilot terms;
- enterprise contract terms;
- budget and hiring triggers.
