# VYTAL House Enterprise Growth OS

GitHub operating system for developing VYTAL House employer-sponsored wellness partnerships across executive wellness, Fort Meade and national-security contractors, cybersecurity and technology firms, healthcare organizations, athletic recovery, first responders, schools, corrections, and institutional partners.

## Core Repository Areas

- `docs/strategy` — market strategy, financial projections, routes, and 90-day launch plan
- `docs/sales` — contact protocol, scripts, program menu, and pricing assumptions
- `docs/compliance` — government, school, healthcare, privacy, and claims guardrails
- `data/templates` — CRM, contacts, pipeline, visits, capacity, and financial templates
- `schemas` — machine-readable account schema
- `scripts` — validation utility
- `dashboard` — lightweight executive dashboard
- `.github` — workflow and collaboration templates

## Important Planning Notice

All pricing, revenue, utilization, conversion, staffing, and cost figures are illustrative planning assumptions until validated by VYTAL House management, legal counsel, insurers, clinical leadership, vendors, and prospective customers.

## Recommended Implementation Order

1. Confirm facility capacity, operating hours, services, and approved wellness claims.
2. Approve corporate membership, pilot, and event pricing.
3. Populate the target-account CRM using verified public sources.
4. Launch the seven-touch founding-partner campaign.
5. Track discovery calls, tours, pilots, proposals, and contracts.

7. Convert successful pilots into annual agreements.

## Data Protection

Do not commit protected health information, employee medical data, student records, Social Security numbers, clearance information, controlled government information, private procurement data, credentials, or nonpublic personal contact data.
