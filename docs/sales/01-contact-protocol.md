# Contact Protocol

## Decision-Maker Hierarchy

### Commercial Employers
1. Chief Human Resources Officer
2. VP or Director of Total Rewards
3. Benefits Manager
4. Employee Wellness Lead
5. Occupational Health
6. Chief People Officer
7. Executive Assistant to CEO or COO
8. Employee Experience or Corporate Events

### Government and Public Sector
1. Public-facing wellness or benefits office
2. Human resources
3. Occupational health
4. Employee assistance program
5. Procurement or contracting office
6. Small-business liaison
7. Public affairs or community relations
8. Union or employee association leadership, where appropriate

### Healthcare
1. Chief Human Resources Officer
2. Chief Nursing Officer
3. Employee health
4. Benefits and Total Rewards
5. Workforce wellbeing
6. Medical staff services
7. Foundation or community partnership office

### Athletics and Schools
1. Athletic Director
2. Principal or school administrator
3. District wellness or risk office
4. Athletic trainer
5. Coach
6. Parent organization only with school approval

### Corrections and First Responders
1. HR or benefits
2. Wellness coordinator
3. Occupational health
4. Training division
5. Risk management
6. Union or employee association
7. Command leadership

## Seven-Touch Cadence

- Day 1: Personalized email
- Day 2: LinkedIn connection
- Day 4: Telephone call
- Day 7: Send one-page pilot concept
- Day 12: Invite to facility tour or executive briefing
- Day 20: Share a relevant use case or outcome framework
- Day 30: Close-the-loop message and quarterly nurture

## Discovery Agenda

1. Workforce profile
2. Existing wellness benefits
3. Priority workforce challenges
4. Participation expectations
5. Procurement path
6. Budget timing
7. Data and privacy requirements
8. Desired pilot population
9. Success measures
10. Decision process and next action

## Contact Recording Standard

Every record should include the contact’s name, role, organization, public business email, public business phone, source URL, verification date, relationship owner, permission or opt-out status, last interaction, and next action.

Never purchase or store sensitive personal information for outreach.
