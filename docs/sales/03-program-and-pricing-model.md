## Contract Controls

Every agreement should define eligible population, included services, usage caps, reservation rules, no-show terms, rollover policy, exclusions, employee cost share, reporting methodology, privacy boundaries, contract term, renewal, termination, insurance, indemnity, and approved marketing statements.
