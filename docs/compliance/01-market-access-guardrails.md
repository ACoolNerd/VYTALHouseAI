# Market Access and Compliance Guardrails

## General

- Use approved, supportable claims.
- Distinguish wellness services from medical diagnosis and treatment.
- Obtain legal review of contracts, waivers, consent forms, marketing, and employer reporting.
- Confirm insurance coverage for every service and customer category.
- Never promise medical outcomes or productivity savings without evidence.
- Report aggregate utilization unless lawful consent and an appropriate data arrangement support more.

## Federal and Defense Markets

- Do not use agency seals, names, insignia, or implied endorsements.
- Do not solicit inside restricted facilities without authorization.
- Confirm whether an opportunity is a commercial employee benefit, subcontract, sponsorship, or procurement.
- Use official procurement and small-business channels when government funds are involved.
- Do not request clearance details, mission information, or controlled information.

## Schools and Minors

- Obtain district or school authorization.
- Require parent or guardian consent where applicable.
- Establish supervision, safeguarding, and emergency protocols.
- Avoid collecting educational or health records unless necessary and legally structured.
- Separate athletic-performance claims from medical treatment.

## Corrections and First Responders

- Use approved agency access procedures.
- Do not access secure areas without authorization.
- Keep employee participation voluntary unless lawfully structured otherwise.
- Avoid diagnostic mental-health claims.
- Coordinate through HR, occupational health, risk, training, or authorized associations.

## Healthcare Organizations

- Do not assume HIPAA applies to every interaction, but design for privacy.
- Avoid receiving identifiable patient information.
- Clarify whether VYTAL House is acting as a vendor, provider, benefit partner, or event operator.
- Execute appropriate agreements before handling protected information.
