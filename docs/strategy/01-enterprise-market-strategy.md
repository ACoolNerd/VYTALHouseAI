# Enterprise Market Strategy

## Commercial Thesis

VYTAL House should combine employer-paid memberships, subsidized memberships, prepaid session banks, executive leadership packages, team recovery days, on-site activations, short pilots, and annual enterprise agreements.

The objective is recurring contracted revenue with strict utilization and capacity controls.

## Priority Segments

### Tier 1 — Fort Meade and National Security Ecosystem

Target Fort Meade tenant organizations, federal civilian employee groups, Department of Defense components, NSA- and USCYBERCOM-adjacent contractors, prime contractors, subcontractors, cyber firms, engineering firms, and cleared-professional associations.

Preferred entry points:

- Contractor HR and Total Rewards
- Employee wellness
- Occupational health
- Benefits leadership
- Veteran and military-affinity groups
- Small-business liaison offices
- Government procurement and partnership offices when public funding is involved

Never imply endorsement by Fort Meade, NSA, USCYBERCOM, DoD, or another agency without written authorization.

### Tier 2 — Cybersecurity and Technology

Target cybersecurity firms, managed security providers, cloud and data companies, software firms, engineering firms, AI companies, data centers, and technology staffing organizations.

Primary workforce needs include burnout, cognitive load, sedentary work, retention, leadership fatigue, shift coverage, and employer-brand differentiation.

### Tier 3 — Healthcare

Target hospitals, health systems, medical groups, nursing organizations, home-health providers, staffing agencies, rehabilitation organizations, physical therapy groups, and physician practices.

Primary offers include nurse recovery, leadership wellness, shift-worker programs, employee appreciation days, prepaid sessions, and annual benefit partnerships.

### Tier 4 — First Responders and Corrections

Target police, fire and rescue, EMS, emergency communications, sheriff offices, correctional facilities, detention centers, probation, and parole organizations.

Use trauma-informed, non-diagnostic language. Do not claim to treat PTSD, injuries, disease, or occupational conditions unless lawfully supported and delivered.

### Tier 5 — Athletics and Schools

Target high-school athletic departments, private schools, colleges, sports clubs, training academies, adult leagues, coaches, athletic trainers, and strength programs.

Programs involving minors require consent, safeguarding, privacy, supervision, and contracting controls.

## Geographic Rings

- Ring 1: Columbia, Elkridge, Hanover, Jessup, Laurel, Savage, Fort Meade, Odenton, Annapolis Junction
- Ring 2: Baltimore, Catonsville, Ellicott City, Glen Burnie, Crofton, Annapolis, Silver Spring, Greenbelt, College Park
- Ring 3: Washington, DC, Northern Virginia, Frederick, and the broader Baltimore–Washington corridor

## Account Scoring

Score each account from 1–5 on employee population, distance, benefits budget, wellness need, decision-maker accessibility, strategic credibility, procurement complexity, pilot potential, renewal potential, and capacity fit.

Prioritize accounts scoring at least 35 out of 50.
