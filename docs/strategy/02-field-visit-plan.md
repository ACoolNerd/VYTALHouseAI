## Visit Protocol

1. Research the organization and identify an appropriate business contact.
2. Request an appointment; do not rely on unannounced access to secured facilities.
3. Carry the executive overview, program menu, pilot offer, facility overview, insurance/compliance summary, business cards, and discovery-form QR code.
4. Ask for a 20-minute discovery call or private VYTAL House stakeholder briefing.
5. Record every visit in the CRM.
6. Send a recap within one business day.
7. Schedule the next action before closing the record.

## Secured Locations

Do not attempt to enter controlled government, correctional, school, hospital, or critical-infrastructure facilities without permission. Begin through public affairs, procurement, HR, benefits, wellness, community relations, or a known contractor sponsor.
