# 90-Day Launch Plan

## Days 1–15 — Foundation

- Approve services and wellness claims
- Approve enterprise pricing
- Confirm facility opening and capacity
- Establish CRM ownership
- Finalize the executive overview and discovery form
- Finalize a pilot agreement template
- Select 100 priority accounts

## Days 16–30 — Contact Build

- Verify 150 business contacts
- Segment accounts by route and offer
- Begin the seven-touch cadence
- Schedule the first 10 discovery meetings
- Hold two private stakeholder briefings

## Days 31–60 — Pilot Conversion

- Complete 20 discovery calls
- Conduct eight facility tours
- Issue six pilot proposals
- Close two to three paid pilots
- Build reporting and feedback processes
- Document objections and revise scripts

## Days 61–90 — Contract and Scale

- Convert the strongest pilots into annual agreements
- Reach 300 verified target contacts
- Produce the first aggregate outcome report
- Build referral partnerships
- Establish a quarterly employer roundtable
- Prepare expansion and hiring decisions
